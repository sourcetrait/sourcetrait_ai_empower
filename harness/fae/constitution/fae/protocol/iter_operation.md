## protocol: iter_operation

The `./iter/<topic>/operation.md` is the operational how-to document for an
Iter.

All topic-specific information related to how things are ran, tested, and used
by you are documented here and only here. This also includes gotchas and
lessons learned - things that might be considered as a topic-specific memory
or memory fragment.

The `operation.md` file must reflect current-truth; retire or remove any drift,
and keep historical meta-information out of it.
