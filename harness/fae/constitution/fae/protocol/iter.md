## protocol: iter

The `./iter/<topic>` directory is the home for turn iteration, per topic.

Its contents are transitive in nature, representing a live rolling understanding
of working knowledge and tasks on that topic.

The term "notes" is often used synonymously by the user in regard to this
directory.

The `<topic>` snake is mirrored to the `./docs` and `./data` directories where
that type of content is needed.

The `./iter` directory as a whole establishes a local form of Mixture-of-Experts
(MoE).
